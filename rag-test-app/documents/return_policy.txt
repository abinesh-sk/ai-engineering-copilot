COMPANY RETURN POLICY

Standard Returns
Most products purchased from our store can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided the item is unused and in its original packaging. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of us receiving the returned item.

Non-Returnable Categories
The following product categories are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged under any circumstances, regardless of the 30-day window: perishable goods, custom-engraved items, digital downloads, gift cards, and Product X (industrial safety equipment). Product X is excluded from standard returns because it falls under safety-certification regulations that require unopened factory sealing to remain valid for resale.

Damaged or Defective Items
If an item arrives damaged or defective, contact support within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damage. Damaged items are eligible for a replacement or refund regardless of category, including Product X, since this falls under warranty rather than the standard return policy.

Return Shipping Costs
Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the return is due to our error (wrong item shipped) or a defective product.
