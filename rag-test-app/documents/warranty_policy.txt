COMPANY WARRANTY POLICY

Standard Warranty
All products except Product X and clearance items carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. This does not cover damage from misuse, accidents, or normal wear.

Product X Extended Warranty
Product X (industrial safety equipment) carries a 3-year extended warranty due to its safety-critical nature, separate from and in addition to the standard 1-year warranty terms. Warranty claims for Product X require the original certification card that shipped with the unit.

Filing a Warranty Claim
To file a warranty claim, submit the product serial number and a description of the defect through the support portal. Approved claims are resolved via repair, replacement, or store credit at our discretion, typically within 10 business days.
