COMPANY SHIPPING POLICY

Standard Shipping
Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days and is free on orders over $50. Orders under $50 incur a flat $6.99 shipping fee.

Expedited Shipping
Expedited shipping takes 2-3 business days for a flat fee of $14.99, available for all products except items in the Oversized category (furniture, appliances), which ship via freight carrier only.

International Shipping
We currently ship to Canada and the United Kingdom only. International orders take 10-14 business days and customs fees, if any, are the responsibility of the recipient.

Order Tracking
Tracking numbers are emailed within 24 hours of a package leaving our warehouse. If tracking has not updated in 5 business days, contact support.
